# QualityCraft

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Malaysian Form 4 and Form 5 students learning introductory software testing. Teachers guide classes and review practical work; administrators maintain content, accounts, rewards, and research settings.

## Product Purpose

Help students read lessons, practise software testing, complete quizzes, and understand their progress. The existing application is the functional authority.

## Operating Context

Students join a class, complete their pre-test, work through sequential modules, try optional practical activities and pass MCQ lesson quizzes with a score of at least 50%, then complete a post-test. Progress, XP, badges, survey responses, and class membership are saved through the existing API.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- React/Vite frontend, Express API, MySQL persistence.
- Preserve all learning content, features, roles, permissions, assessment ordering, rewards, and existing records.
- Current redesign scope confirmed by the user: sign-in and all student, teacher and administrator screens. Preserve the existing system structure.

## Current Curriculum

The active course has three ordered modules: Foundations of Quality (Easy), Test Case Design (Moderate), and Testing Strategies (Hard). Each has six multiple-choice questions; three correct answers is a 50% pass. Module completion and next-module access depend only on the quiz score. Reading is optional. Practical activity submissions are optional and never block quizzes or completion. Other modules are hidden while historical attempts, submissions and badges are preserved. Pre-test and post-test understanding surveys remain separate from lesson quizzes.
